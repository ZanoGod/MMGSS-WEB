Our recruitment process

From job order to
departure to Japan.

A structured 15-step recruitment and employment-support process designed to guide candidates and Japanese employers from the first job order through post-arrival support.

15 steps Myanmar Japan View current opportunities
How it works

A clear path at every stage.

Each stage has a defined purpose, from receiving the employer's requirements to preparing candidates, completing required procedures, and supporting workers after arrival in Japan.

01
Step 01

Receive Job Order

Receive a job order and recruitment requirements from a Japanese employer.

02
Step 02

Job Announcement & Candidate Recruitment

Publish the job vacancy on our official page and begin recruiting suitable candidates.

03
Step 03

CV Collection & Document Screening

Collect candidates’ CVs and required documents.

04
Step 04

In-House Japanese Conversation Test

Conduct a Japanese conversation (Kaiwa) test at our agency and select qualified candidates.

05
Step 05

CV Submission to Japan

Submit the CVs of candidates who pass our Agency’s Kaiwa test to the Japanese employer for further consideration.

06
Step 06

Employer Interview

Arrange and conduct interviews between the selected candidates and the Japanese employer.

07
Step 07

Employment Contract

Successful candidates sign an employment contract with the Japanese employer.

08
Step 08

Demand Application

Submit the required demand documents to the relevant labor authorities in Myanmar.

09
Step 09

Certificate of Eligibility (COE) Application

Proceed with the Certificate of Eligibility (COE) application in Japan.

10
Step 10

Visa Application

Apply for the appropriate visa at the relevant visa application center.

11
Step 11

Standard Labor Contract

Complete the required labor contract procedures with the relevant labor authorities in Myanmar.

12
Step 12

OWIC Card Application

Apply for the Overseas Worker Identification Card (OWIC).

13
Step 13

Pre-Departure Orientation

Provide pre-departure orientation covering work conditions, workplace rules, life in Japan, and important information for living and working in Japan.

14
Step 14

Departure to Japan

Complete the final departure procedures and travel to Japan.

15
Step 15

Post-Arrival Support

Provide necessary support and follow-up assistance after the workers arrive in Japan.
